ARCH-02 — EXTERNAL REUSE, ALIGNMENT, AND IRI POLICY — DLCRO v0.1
Status: alignment baseline frozen in Pull 05.
Objective: decide import vs reuse-by-reference vs mapping for external resources and establish the namespace/IRI policy before OWL-01.
Binding principle: DLCRO domain modules may depend on other DLCRO modules according to the ARCH-01 DAG, but they must not depend at load time on mutable external ontologies.

1. CANONICAL IDENTITY AND NAMESPACE
Name: Dental Light-Curing and Radiometry Ontology.
Acronym: DLCRO.
Recommended local prefix: dlcro for the aggregator; module prefixes may be dlcro-core, dlcro-lcu, dlcro-rad, dlcro-radiometer, dlcro-met, dlcro-ctx, dlcro-mat, dlcro-qi, and dlcro-align.
Target persistent namespace: https://w3id.org/dlcro/.
Planned base Ontology IRI: https://w3id.org/dlcro.
Module IRIs: https://w3id.org/dlcro/core, /radiometry, /context, /metrology, /lcu, /radiometer, /material, /qi, and /alignment.
Planned Version IRI: https://w3id.org/dlcro/0.1.0-dev/<module> for the first skeleton; the public release must replace dev with the approved SemVer version.
W3ID still needs to be reserved; therefore, this policy freezes the IRI form but does not claim that the redirect is already active.

2. GLOBAL EXTERNAL-DEPENDENCY RULE
No DLCRO domain module will use owl:imports for external ontologies in v0.1-dev.
Stable external terms will be used through direct IRIs (reuse-by-reference).
Formal mappings between DLCRO and external classes/properties are placed in DLCRO-Alignment.
A future “full/bundled” distribution may provide external snapshots or a controlled import graph for testing without changing the canonical core.
Internal owl:imports among DLCRO modules are allowed and must follow the ARCH-01 DAG exactly.

3. SSN/SOSA — DECISION
Normative baseline: Semantic Sensor Network Ontology / SOSA Recommendation of 19 October 2017.
The SSN 2023 edition published as a Working Draft on 24 September 2026 will be monitored, but it is not a normative dependency of v0.1.
Strategy: MAPPING in DLCRO-Alignment; no external owl:imports.
Approved formal mappings for v0.1: DLCRO:MeasurementActivity rdfs:subClassOf sosa:Observation; DLCRO:Detector rdfs:subClassOf sosa:Sensor; DLCRO:DentalRadiometer rdfs:subClassOf ssn:System; DLCRO:LightCuringUnit rdfs:subClassOf ssn:System.
Do not declare LightCuringUnit owl:equivalentClass sosa:Actuator or ExposureEvent owl:equivalentClass sosa:Actuation in this release; such equivalence would be stronger than the available conceptual evidence.
Reference namespaces: http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/ and http://www.w3.org/ns/ssn/.

4. QUDT 3.5.2 — DECISION
Strategy: REUSE-BY-REFERENCE; no owl:imports.
Use qudt:QuantityValue, qudt:hasQuantityKind, qudt:unit, qudt:numericValue, and the quantity kinds approved in MET-01 directly.
Frozen bindings: quantitykind:RadiantFlux, quantitykind:Irradiance, quantitykind:SpectralIrradiance, and quantitykind:RadiantExposure.
Use non-versioned QUDT term namespaces in data/axioms and record QUDT 3.5.2 as the release baseline/provenance version.
Do not replicate units or quantity kinds as DLCRO classes.
OM 2.0 remains only a secondary crosswalk; no QUDT↔OM equivalence will be issued in v0.1 without term-by-term audit.

5. PROV-O — DECISION
Baseline: W3C Recommendation of 30 April 2013.
Strategy: REUSE-BY-REFERENCE + MAPPING; no owl:imports.
Use prov:Entity, prov:Activity, prov:Agent, and provenance properties directly when sufficient.
Approved mappings: ExposureEvent, MeasurementActivity, CalibrationEvent, VerificationEvent, and MaintenanceEvent are formal candidates for rdfs:subClassOf prov:Activity in DLCRO-Alignment; EvidenceBundle and digital/informational artifacts or results may be related to prov:Entity without turning metrological traceability into provenance.
PROV-O alone never satisfies CQ-11 on metrological traceability.

6. SI REFERENCE POINT 1.0.0 — DECISION
Strategy: AUTHORITATIVE REFERENCE / MAPPING; no import.
Use the SI Reference Point as an authoritative digital reference to the SI and as an interoperability anchor for SI units/identifiers when mappings are available and audited.
QUDT remains DLCRO’s operational quantity/value/unit pattern.
Do not duplicate SI units locally or declare automatic equivalences between all QUDT and SIRP identifiers without term-by-term verification.

7. D-SI, DCC, AND SIS — DECISION
Strategy: INTEROPERABILITY TARGET / MAPPING.
D-SI and DCC will not be imported as if the entire ecosystem were an OWL ontology.
DLCRO results, uncertainties, and certificates should be transformable/mappable to digital metrology structures; exact mappings will be added only when IRIs/schemas and fields have been tested.
SIS may be mapped selectively when it provides stable and useful RDF terms; it is not a mandatory dependency of v0.1.

8. OMT v1.0 — DECISION
Strategy: CANDIDATE MAPPING, no import.
The publication by Wang et al. (2024) demonstrates an ontology for measurement terminology applied to Digital Calibration Certificates, but DLCRO will not adopt global owl:equivalentClass/properties at this stage.
Local VIM-like terms already frozen in MET-01 may receive individual mappings in a later release after audit of IRI, license, definitions, and cardinalities.

9. OM 2.0 — DECISION
Strategy: SECONDARY CROSSWALK ONLY.
QUDT is the primary quantities/units model. OM will not be imported or used in parallel as a primary model.
OM mappings will be considered only for interoperability with external datasets that already use OM.

10. DCTERMS AND SKOS — DECISION
Strategy: REUSE-BY-REFERENCE.
dcterms may be used for title, description, creator, source, license, and date metadata when appropriate.
SKOS may be used for weak conceptual annotations/mappings when rdfs:subClassOf, equivalentClass, or equivalentProperty would be semantically excessive.
Do not import the complete vocabularies.

11. MATERIAL/CHEMISTRY — DECISION
No chemical/biomedical ontology is a mandatory dependency of DLCRO v0.1.
Strategy: DEFERRED MAPPING.
PhotoinitiatingSystem, Photoinitiator, and Coinitiator remain minimal local terms until entity-driven research and real interoperability needs justify expansion.
Do not create chemical equivalences without substance/structure identifiers and an authoritative source.

12. ISO 10650:2026 AND ISO 4049:2019
These are normative/documentary sources, not ontologies to import.
Strategy: REFERENCE-AS-DOCUMENT through DLCRO-QI.
StandardDocument, Requirement, and ApplicabilityAssertion should reference edition, clause, and source when full institutional text is used.
The presence of a requirement does not imply a true ConformityAssertion.

13. DLCRO-ALIGNMENT — MINIMUM v0.1 CONTENT
The Alignment module must contain only approved mappings and decision metadata; it must not contain domain concepts.
Each mapping must record the external resource, considered version/baseline, semantic strength used (subClassOf, subPropertyOf, exact/close match, or equivalence), rationale, and source.
owl:equivalentClass and owl:equivalentProperty require specific human review and will not be used as shortcuts.
Experimental mappings must be separated from approved mappings, preferably through status/annotation and test fixtures.

14. INTERNAL IMPORT GRAPH
DLCRO-Radiometry owl:imports DLCRO-Core.
DLCRO-Context owl:imports DLCRO-Core and DLCRO-Radiometry.
DLCRO-Metrology owl:imports DLCRO-Core, DLCRO-Radiometry, and DLCRO-Context.
DLCRO-LCU owl:imports DLCRO-Core, DLCRO-Radiometry, and DLCRO-Context.
DLCRO-Radiometer owl:imports DLCRO-Core, DLCRO-Radiometry, DLCRO-Context, and DLCRO-Metrology.
DLCRO-Material owl:imports DLCRO-Core, DLCRO-Radiometry, and DLCRO-Metrology.
DLCRO-QI owl:imports DLCRO-Core.
DLCRO-Alignment owl:imports all DLCRO modules required by its mappings, but no domain module imports Alignment.
The aggregator https://w3id.org/dlcro may import the domain modules; Alignment may be included in the complete package, while the core distribution may be offered without it.

15. VERSIONING AND REPRODUCIBILITY
External prefixes/term IRIs should point to canonical non-versioned namespaces when that is the resource’s established practice; the external version used in validation must be recorded in release metadata/manifest.
External dependency snapshots, when required for reproducible QA, should remain in the validation/release package and must not redefine the original IRIs.
Any future change in an external baseline that alters inferences requires a new governance item and a reasoner regression test.

16. ACCEPTANCE CRITERION
Each prioritized external resource has an explicit decision: reuse-by-reference, mapping, authoritative reference, interoperability target, secondary crosswalk, deferred mapping, or reference-as-document.
The DLCRO namespace and internal import graph are defined.
The minimum content of DLCRO-Alignment is frozen.
ARCH-02 satisfies the backlog item and unblocks OWL-01.
