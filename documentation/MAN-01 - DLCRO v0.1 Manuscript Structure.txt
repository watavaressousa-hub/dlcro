MAN-01 — DLCRO v0.1 MANUSCRIPT STRUCTURE
Status: scientific outline frozen in Pull 04. This document organizes the manuscript but does not anticipate results that have not yet been obtained.
Editorial objective: present DLCRO as a reuse-first semantic/computational contribution that integrates dental light-curing units, radiometers, radiometry, metrology, material, and evidence while respecting the SCI-01 claim ceiling.

1. PROVISIONAL TITLE
English: DLCRO: A reuse-first modular ontology for dental light-curing units, radiometers, and radiometric-metrological evidence.
Portuguese: DLCRO: uma ontologia modular reuse-first para fotopolimerizadores, radiômetros dentais e evidência radiométrico-metrológica.
The final title should mention validation, a knowledge graph, or demonstrated interoperability only after the corresponding results have been completed.

2. ABSTRACT — STRUCTURE
Background: fragmentation of LCU, exposure, radiometer, geometry, spectrum, material, and metrological-evidence data.
Objective: develop and semantically evaluate a modular OWL 2 ontology that integrates these elements without collapsing indication, result, measurand, geometry, uncertainty, and traceability.
Methods: source-guided scoping, reuse-first development, Competency Questions, modular architecture, OWL 2, reasoner, SPARQL, SHACL, and a demonstrative case.
Results: fill only after implementation/validation; report modules, terms/reuse, CQ coverage, logical/SHACL/SPARQL tests, and demonstration without inventing metrics.
Conclusion: limit the conclusion to what the semantic/computational tests actually demonstrate.

3.1 Context of dental light-curing and the need to interpret optical output using spectrum, geometry, and time.
3.2 Limitations of isolated radiometer readings and the distinction between indication and measurement result.
3.3 Fragmentation across dental sources, radiometry, metrology, materials, and quality.
3.4 Related work: SSN/SOSA, QUDT, PROV-O, digital SI, and metrology resources; make explicit that the contribution is not a claim of universal absence of ontologies.
3.5 Specific integration gap and SCI-01 Research Question.
3.6 Article contributions: modular model, domain integration, CQs/validation, reuse-first policy, and demonstrative scenario.

6. DISCUSSION
6.1 Principal semantic contribution: domain-specific integration of dental light-curing with radiometric and metrological foundations.
6.2 Why indication is not measurement truth: implications for radiometer representation.
6.3 Why spectrum, geometry, and spatial distribution matter: connection with the LCU literature.
6.4 Material/photoinitiator context: usefulness of spectral compatibility without turning it into an automatic predictor of cure.
6.5 Reuse-first and interoperability: benefits and limitations of SSN/SOSA, QUDT, PROV-O, and the digital-metrology ecosystem.
6.6 Validation significance: distinguish parsing, logical consistency, SHACL completeness, and the ability to answer CQs.
6.7 Limitations: absence of automatic clinical validation; minimal chemical scope; dependence on data quality; external mappings remain subject to evolution; open-world knowledge.
6.8 Future work: validation with real data, additional alignments, DCC/digital metrology, expansion of materials, and quality profiles.

7. CONCLUSION
Answer the Research Question directly only with evidence produced by implementation and validation.
Do not conclude that DLCRO improves clinical outcomes, that a radiometer is suitable for any use, or that a device/material is conforming.
Emphasize, if demonstrated, semantic interoperability, queryability, provenance traceability, and integration of metrological evidence.

10. EDITORIAL CLAIM CEILING
Allowed when demonstrated: semantic formalization, modularity, reuse, logical consistency, queryability, CQ coverage, SHACL profile validation, provenance integration, and representation of metrological evidence.
Not allowed without additional studies: clinical accuracy, cure adequacy, degree-of-conversion prediction, depth-of-cure prediction, technology superiority, regulatory conformity, validity of a real calibration, or fitness for purpose.
Terms such as “validated ontology” must always be qualified by the type of validation performed.

11. DECISION
The outline separates Methods, Results, and Discussion and limits claims to the semantic/computational level, satisfying the backlog item.
MAN-01 remains frozen as the v0.1 writing structure; full manuscript writing will be activated after sufficient implementation/validation results are available.
