DLCRO v0.1-RC1 — Dental Light-Curing and Radiometry Ontology
Status: Release Candidate 1. Not a final stable release.
Scope: modular OWL 2 ontology for dental light-curing units, dental radiometers, radiometric/metrological evidence, context, material/photoinitiator information, QI and alignments.
Package layout: ontology/, validation/, examples/, documentation/, release-metadata/.
Validation status: VAL-01 reasoning/profile PASS; VAL-02 15/15 Must CQs executable and non-empty on demo KG; VAL-03 positive/negative SHACL fixtures; VAL-04 pySHACL 0.40.1 full-engine confirmation PASS.
Strong OWL constructs remain intentionally absent in RC1: cardinalities, owl:Restriction, disjointness, property chains, nominals and strong equivalence axioms.
Namespace target: https://w3id.org/dlcro/ — reservation pending; do not treat as active redirect until accepted in perma-id/w3id.org.
Default aggregator: ontology/dlcro.ttl. Alignment module is intentionally not imported by the default aggregator.
Demo data are synthetic validation fixtures and must not be interpreted as clinical performance, calibration, conformity or fitness-for-purpose evidence.
Licensing: ontology/data/documentation CC BY 4.0; validation scripts MIT.
Release date: 2026-09-26.
